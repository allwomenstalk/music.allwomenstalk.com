# 7 Empowering Female Empowerment Songs Every Woman Needs on Her Playlist

Women need to stick together, and building a playlist of women empowerment songs is one of the best ways to boost your confidence and celebrate sisterhood. The right music can transform your mood, ignite your energy, and remind you of your inner strength. Whether you need inspiration during a workout or a tune to blast while getting ready, female empowerment songs deliver the ultimate motivation. Here are seven of the most catchy songs about women empowerment that you will want to play on repeat:

## 1. Salute by Little Mix

You won't find anything with a better beat than this military-inspired anthem! The track is all about women standing united and recognizing that we need to stick together. [Little Mix](https://www.little-mix.com) delivers a powerful message that women are far more than just pretty faces; they are strong, capable human beings who can conquer any challenge. Much like [Beyoncé's fierce energy](#2), the intense choreography in the music video makes this song an unforgettable anthem for women empowerment.

## 2. Run the World by Beyonce

You cannot discuss songs about female empowerment without mentioning [Beyoncé](https://www.beyonce.com). While she has plenty of empowering tracks in her discography, "Run the World (Girls)" stands out as one of the most explosive songs about strong women ever recorded. The lyrics remind us that women drive society, business, and culture forward. Men might think they hold all the control, but this anthem reminds everyone who truly keeps everything running.

## 3. Girls by Jennifer Lopez

From the very first line of the track, you will see why this song earns a spot among top empowering female songs. It celebrates women who work hard, make their own money, and take complete ownership of their lives. Taking care of business does not mean you cannot let loose and enjoy a night out with your friends. Having fun and celebrating your achievements is an essential part of living life to the fullest.

## 4. Secrets by Mary Lambert

This refreshingly honest song encourages women to let go of self-doubt and stop worrying about imperfections. There is no reason to pretend to be someone else when you are already remarkable as you are. Rather than hiding your secrets, embrace your authentic self. Opening up about who you really are brings a genuine sense of freedom and self-love. If you enjoy uplifting messages like this, you will also love [Keri Hilson's confidence-boosting anthem](#5).

## 5. Pretty Girl Rock by Keri Hilson

There is absolutely nothing wrong with owning your beauty and feeling proud of who you are. Keri Hilson encourages listeners to step into the spotlight without fear of judgment. Self-assurance is not cockiness; it is healthy self-respect. Loving your look and walking with confidence inspires other women to feel good about themselves too.

**Key Takeaways for Your Empowerment Playlist:**

- Choose songs with uplifting beats to boost your workout or morning routine.
- Focus on lyrics that emphasize independence, self-worth, and resilience.
- Share your favorite anthems with friends to spread positive energy.

## 6. Fly by Nicki Minaj and Rihanna

You have likely heard this hit on the radio, and its powerful message remains timeless. The song captures the spirit of women who overcome obstacles and rise above adversity. Both Nicki Minaj and [Rihanna](https://www.rihannanow.com) embody strength and determination, making this collaboration a standout choice among songs to empower women when facing tough times. For a country-style perspective on overcoming life's hurdles, check out [Martina McBride's classic track](#7).

## 7. This One’s for the Girls by Martina McBride

This timeless country hit resonates with listeners at every stage of life. [Martina McBride](https://www.martinamcbride.com) delivers a comforting reminder that growing up, making mistakes, and navigating change are all part of the journey. No matter your age, this song offers reassurance that you are strong enough to weather any storm and come out thriving on the other side.

Being a woman is something to celebrate every day. We live in an era rich with opportunities to pursue our dreams, build fulfilling careers, and lift each other up. Music is one of the most powerful tools to keep that positive energy flowing. What other empowering female songs have you heard that never fail to lift your spirits?
