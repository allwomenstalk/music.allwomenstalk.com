# 7 Legendary Blues Guitarists Every Music Fan Must Hear

Listening to blues music elicits an incredible array of emotions, and my love for this genre has led me to discover some of the finest blues guitarists the world has ever heard. While my first romance with music started with rock ‘n’ roll, I quickly became hypnotized by the raw feel of the blues. The legendary figures featured here represent some of the best blues guitarists of all time, each bringing a unique style, tone, and personal expression to the stage. Whether you are looking for classic acoustic Delta blues or heavy rock blues guitarists, these artists defined generations of music. Though several are sadly no longer with us, their legendary recordings ensure that the spirit of the blues remains vibrant and alive.

## 1. Robert Johnson

![Robert Johnson](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zv/bh/mv4pwhr2_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [The Complete Recordings](https://music.apple.com/ie/album/the-complete-recordings/325719762)

Robert Johnson remains surrounded by myth and is universally acknowledged as a foundational influence by generations of American blues guitarists. Johnson developed an extraordinary guitar style that creates the illusion of two guitars playing simultaneously—a complex thumb-and-finger technique that countless musicians have tried to replicate. Folklore claims he sold his soul to the devil at a Mississippi crossroads in exchange for his haunting musical genius. Johnson passed away at just 27 years of age, and his mysterious life and tragic end only heightened the legacy of this pioneering, famous blues guitar player.

## 2. Buddy Guy

![Buddy Guy](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/a2/oc/zt3utn2r_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [Damn Right, I've Got the Blues](https://goo.gl/ak6Syj) | Visit [Official Buddy Guy Website](https://www.buddyguy.net/)

Buddy Guy is a living legend of Chicago blues, famous for electrifying and flamboyant stage performances delivered to audiences worldwide. His untamed style radiates raw energy, and the passion in his playing is unmistakable with every fiery string bend. His showmanship and explosive tone directly inspired later innovators, including legendary [rock blues guitarists like Stevie Ray Vaughan](#3). Eric Clapton famously described Buddy Guy as the greatest guitar player alive, a title well supported by his 1991 Grammy-winning classic, _Damn Right, I’ve Got the Blues_.

What makes Buddy Guy's sound so iconic:

- High-gain guitar tone with dramatic dynamic shifts
- Expressive, wide string bends and wild vibrato
- Unpredictable, show-stopping stage presence

## 3. Stevie Ray Vaughan (SRV)

![Stevie Ray Vaughan (SRV)](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jb/06/m16kqvq6_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [The Essential Stevie Ray Vaughan](https://music.apple.com/us/album/essential-stevie-ray-vaughan/193063151) | Learn more at [Official SRV Site](https://www.srvofficial.com/)

Stevie Ray Vaughan is another giant among popular blues guitarists who left us far too soon. Known for his unmistakable Fender Stratocaster attack, heavy gauge strings, and thunderous tone, SRV brought incredible power to both blistering solos and heart-wrenching [slow blues rooted in Robert Johnson's legacy](#1). His joyful, energetic playing revitalized mainstream interest in blues-rock throughout the 1980s before his tragic passing in a helicopter crash at age 35.

## 4. Gary Moore

![Gary Moore](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/sf/o1/rm3dgge2_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [Still Got The Blues](https://music.apple.com/us/album/still-got-the-blues/723401781)

Gary Moore was an Irish guitar virtuoso who achieved stardom with hard rock outfit Thin Lizzy before establishing himself as one of the standout guitarists like Gary Moore fans constantly praise in blues rock circles. He moved effortlessly between high-speed dexterity and soulful sustained notes, showcasing incredible versatility on slow blues tracks. His 1990 release _Still Got the Blues_ re-anchored his career in authentic blues phrasing, matching the intensity of icons like [Buddy Guy](#2). Though he passed away in 2011, his passionate musical imprint lives on.

## 5. Joe Bonamassa

![Joe Bonamassa](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/un/zu/ra8pw49j_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [Sloe Gin](https://music.apple.com/us/album/sloe-gin/318564398) | Official site: [jbonamassa.com](https://jbonamassa.com/)

Joe Bonamassa stands out as one of the most prolific modern masters among great blues guitarists. Playing with jaw-dropping precision, he seamlessly fuses traditional blues licks with rock power reminiscent of [Stevie Ray Vaughan](#3). Constantly touring, writing, and recording, Bonamassa coaxes endless tonal versatility from his vintage Gibson Les Paul collection. His work ethic and technical expertise make him a central driver of the genre today.

## 6. Kenny Wayne Shepherd (KWS)

![Kenny Wayne Shepherd (KWS)](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pw/c0/d1bqglfh_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [Leadbetter Heights](https://goo.gl/qvkp4x) | Official website: [kennywayneshepherd.net](https://www.kennywayneshepherd.net/)

Kenny Wayne Shepherd emerged as a prodigy in the mid-1990s, releasing his acclaimed debut album _Leadbetter Heights_ at just 18 years old. As a prominent American blues guitarist, KWS has spent decades producing energetic, genre-defining blues-rock. Armed with his signature Fender Stratocaster, Shepherd delivers melodic lead work and rhythmic grooves that appeal to fans of [Gary Moore](#4) and classical rock-blues guitarists alike.

## 7. Matt Schofield

![Matt Schofield](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/uy/jm/j9zrnxg6_255x255.jpg)

**Must-listen album on Apple Music:** [Ear to the Ground](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ear-to-the-ground/255495583)

Matt Schofield has been recognized by _Guitarist_ magazine as one of the UK’s most exciting blues guitarists. His playing blends classic blues phrasing with sophisticated jazz harmony, resulting in fluid licks and dynamic tone. Much like modern peer [Joe Bonamassa](#5), Schofield brings a fresh perspective to guitar-driven music while honoring legacy blues traditions.

Listening to these guitar giants takes you on an emotional journey from buoyant energy to quiet reflection. That depth of feeling is why the blues remains timeless. Which famous blues guitar players have influenced your own playlist?
