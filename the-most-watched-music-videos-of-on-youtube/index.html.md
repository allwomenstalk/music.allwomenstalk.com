# Top 10 Most Watched Music Videos of 2014 on YouTube

The most watched music videos of 2014 were all videos to songs that exploded on global music charts and dominated pop culture. These artists understood the magic of creating entertaining, high-concept visuals that fans could watch on loop without getting tired of pressing the replay button. From blockbuster pop spectacles to global dance anthems, 2014 delivered unforgettable visuals that redefined what it means to go viral on YouTube. Whether you're revisiting nostalgia or discovering these records for the first time, here is the breakdown of the top 10 most viewed music video on youtube from that incredible year in music history.

## 1. Dark Horse

At a staggering 752.4 million views in 2014, Katy Perry's "Dark Horse" featuring Juicy J tops the list of most watched music videos of 2014! Released in February 2014, the Egyptian-themed visual quickly became one of the pop icon's biggest hits. Beyond its mesmerizing colors and catchy trap-pop beat, the video stirred up significant online conversation. Just weeks after its debut, the video was subtly edited following a petition by viewers who objected to a symbol in the video as [blasphemous](https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/katy-perrys--dark-horse-3188452). That viral controversy only amplified its reach, cementing its place among the most viewed music videos of all time on [the official YouTube upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0KSOMA3QBU0).

## 2. Bailando

Enrique Iglesias staged an undeniable global comeback in the summer of 2014 with his hit single "Bailando," featuring Descemer Bueno and Gente De Zona! Uploaded in April 2014, the vibrant Santo Domingo street party video managed to rack up approximately 639.5 million views by the end of the year. The track proved that Latin pop possessed unmatched crossover power on global streaming platforms, making it a contender for the most streamed music video of that summer season.

## 3. Can't Remember to Forget You

Shakira teamed up with Rihanna for one of the most talked-about pop collaborations of the decade. As [rihanna most viewed song on youtube](#9) collab candidate from that era, the catchy reggae-rock beat was backed by an undeniably captivating video that pulled in 456.3 million views. The star power of both icons ensured the song dominated radios and playlists for months on end.

## 4. All about That Bass

It is safe to say that 2014 marked the breakthrough of Meghan Trainor. Her body-positive anthem "All About That Bass" captured instant pop culture fame, and its pastel-toned video kept accumulating massive viewership after its June release. Perfectly timed as a summer radio hit, the video achieved over 451 million views that year and eventually surged past multi-billion view milestones on [music charts worldwide](https://www.billboard.com).

## 5. La La La

Shakira achieved the impressive feat of claiming two spots in the top 10 most watched youtube music video rankings for 2014! Her energetic "La La La (Brazil 2014)" featuring Brazilian artist Carlinhos Brown served as an unofficial anthem for the 2014 FIFA World Cup. Accumulating 433.4 million views, the video gained extra momentum from star-studded footballer cameos, including memorable appearances by Lionel Messi and Neymar. You can compare this with [Shakira's earlier collab with Rihanna](#3) to see how her music videos dominated the charts that year.

Key elements that made these 2014 music videos go viral on YouTube:

- **High-Concept Visuals:** Story-driven themes like Katy Perry's Egyptian aesthetic and Iggy Azalea's _Clueless_ homage.
- **Global Sports & Event Tie-Ins:** Anthems timed with massive global events like the 2014 FIFA World Cup.
- **Star-Studded Collaborations:** Powerhouse duos combining fanbases across pop, hip-hop, and Latin genres.

## 6. Wiggle

Jason Derulo featuring Snoop Dogg brought unforgettable party vibes with "Wiggle." Gathering 432.1 million views in 2014, the playful and infectious music video firmly re-established Derulo on mainstream music radar screens. Check out more [music trends and pop countdowns](https://music.allwomenstalk.com/) across our site for more hit breakdowns.

## 7. Shake It off

Although Taylor Swift was arguably the most discussed artist of 2014 following the release of her blockbuster album _1989_, her lead single "Shake It Off" took the #7 spot on the year's YouTube view leaderboard. Racking up 431.5 million views during its release year, the fun dance video highlighted Swift's transition into full pop superstardom.

## 8. Chandelier

Sia's emotional masterpiece "Chandelier" introduced audiences to an extraordinary performance by young dancer Maddie Ziegler. The raw artistic choreography in a simple apartment setting resonated worldwide, earning 420.7 million views in 2014. The clip remains one of the most artistically acclaimed releases on the [official Sia Vevo channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vjPBrBU-TM).

## 9. Fancy

Iggy Azalea's 90s nostalgia homage to the movie _Clueless_ propelled her and collaborator Charli XCX into mega-stardom. "Fancy" dominated radio stations and summer soundtracks throughout 2014, pulling in 416 million views. It stands as a defining pop-cultural milestone for youtube most watched music videos of that era.

## 10. Anaconda

Nicki Minaj closed out the top 10 with her unforgettable, viral sensation "Anaconda." Generating 354.8 million views in 2014, the bold and unapologetic video broke 24-hour Vevo record metrics at the time and sparked endless internet commentary.
