# 7 Female Artists Whose Music is Pure Poetry: Most Poetic Singers

All music is art, but sometimes an artist writes or sings a line that is pure poetry. So who are the **female artists whose music is pure poetry**?

These are the type of songs that you listen to as a writer and think, “I wish I had thought of that line!” When listening to female poet singers, you realize that lyricism at its highest level transcends simple rhyme schemes. It captures raw emotion, vivid imagery, and profound human truth.

I’ve put together a list of a few female artists/bands with lead female vocalists whose songs are poetry, who string words together in beautiful lines that can speak to any woman’s soul. Here they are - the **female artists whose music is pure poetry**.

Key elements that define these poetic music artists include:

- **Vivid Metaphors:** Transforming everyday experiences into striking visual and emotional imagery.
- **Raw Emotional Vulnerability:** Crafting honest lyrics that resonate deeply with listener experiences.
- **Rhythmic Lyricism:** Balancing cadence and phrasing so words flow like classic verse.

## 1. Adele

“When the world feels so cruel, and your heart makes you feel like a fool, I will be, I will be, your remedy.” - Remedy

Adele has long been recognized among the most poetic singers of her generation. Her ability to translate devastating heartbreak and devotion into universal anthems makes her a prime example of singers who are poets. You can learn more about her discography and background on her [official Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele).

## 2. Halestorm

“Put your lighter in the air and lead me back home.” - Break In

Fronted by Lzzy Hale, Halestorm showcases how rock vocalists can also be famous musician poets. The vulnerability in songs like "Break In" demonstrates how hard-hitting instrumentation paired with delicate lyricism elevates female song artists. Explore more of their music on [Halestorm's official page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halestorm).

## 3. Halsey

“You were a vision in the morning when the light came through. I know I’ve only felt religion when I’ve lied with you.” - Colors

Halsey is a modern female poet singer whose lyrics blend alternative pop with raw literary storytelling. Much like [Adele's soulful remedy](#1), Halsey explores intimate devotion and heartbreak through vivid color symbolism. Discover her latest work at [Halsey's official website](https://www.iamhalsey.com).

## 4. Icon for Hire

“Tryna make my misery just a piece of my history, a little less victim a little more victory.” - Happy Hurts

The icon for hire happy hurts lyrics capture the struggle for mental health and resilience with striking rhythmic precision. Ariel Bloomer's songwriting stands out among trending female artists for its unfiltered honesty and poetic strength.

## 5. Zella Day

“Woven in the fabric of your tapestry, cover me in honeysuckle memories. Compass point you home, calling out from the east, compass points you anywhere, closer to me.” - Compass

Zella Day creates lush, atmospheric landscapes through her lyrics. Her songwriting paints detailed imagery, making her one of the most poetic music artists active today. Like [Halsey's vivid color imagery](#3), Zella Day uses nature and geography to express emotion.

## 6. Kesha

“You brought the flames and you put me through hell, I had to learn how to fight for myself, and we both know all the truth I could tell, I’ll just say this. I wish you farewell. I hope you’re somewhere praying, praying, I hope your soul is changing, changing.” - Praying

In "Praying", Kesha delivered one of the most emotionally powerful ballads of the decade, proving her place among poet singers who channel personal redemption into art. Echoing [Halestorm's anthem of resilience](#2), Kesha turns survival into triumph. Visit [Kesha's official site](https://www.keshasparty.com) for more on her artistic journey and music.

## 7. Little Mix

“Am I still not good enough? Am I still not worth that much? I’m sorry for the way my life turned out, sorry for the smile I’m wearing now. Guess I’m still not good enough.” - Good Enough

Little Mix demonstrates that pop groups can produce deeply poetic lyrics about self-worth and vulnerability. Similar to [Zella Day's introspective tone](#5), "Good Enough" connects with listeners on a personal level, securing their spot among notable female artist groups whose music touches the heart.
